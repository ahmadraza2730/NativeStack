How a project works

  1. Tell us the problem

    Describe what you need in a short message or call.

  2. Scope and quote

    You get a written scope, a fixed price, and a delivery date.

  3. Build in milestones

    You see working software at each milestone and give feedback early.

  4. Hand over

    You receive the finished app, the source code, and support after delivery.
